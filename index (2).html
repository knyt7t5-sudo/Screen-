Shorts cutter (your own video file)
Pick a video to begin.
Shorts are recorded while the video plays, so a 30 second short takes about 30 seconds to make. Keep this page open and the screen on. The file is saved as WebM (or MP4 if your browser supports it).

A mirror screen is a copy of the master screen. Choose how many mirrors you want from the dropdown (20, 40, 100, 200, 400 and so on, all the way up to 180000); they are numbered from 1. Just keep scrolling down: new mirrors start up as you reach them and sync with the master automatically, and screens far behind you are paused and unloaded so your phone doesn't freeze. Use the number box to jump to any mirror that is already unlocked. Mirrors also unlock slowly over time: every 2 to 3 seconds (random), 5 to 10 new ones (random) appear, paced so that reaching 180000 takes at least one full day. Keep this page open; your progress is saved on this device. For YouTube, play, pause and seeking are all copied (mirrors stay muted). For links like Instagram or TikTok, every screen opens the same link, but scrolling or tapping in the master cannot be copied.
